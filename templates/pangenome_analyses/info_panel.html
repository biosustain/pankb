Distribution of QC metrics for genomes that passed all filters in {{ speciesData.species }}:
Completeness (%)
Estimated genome completeness based on single-copy marker genes. Higher values indicate more complete assemblies.
Contamination (%)
Estimated proportion of sequences from other organisms. Lower values indicate purer genome assemblies.
N50
Assembly contiguity metric: the contig length at which 50% of the genome is covered. Higher N50 indicates less fragmented assemblies.
#Contigs
Number of contiguous sequences in the assembly. Fewer contigs generally indicate better assembly quality.
GC Content (%)
Proportion of guanine and cytosine bases. Outliers may indicate contamination or sequencing bias.
